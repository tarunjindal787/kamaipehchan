Verifiable income and credit passport for multi-employer informal workers

A single, lender-ready record of income built from real, confirmed payments — not a self-reported form.

Workers earning across three to five unlinked employers are effectively invisible to lenders — not because they earn too little, but because that income is fragmented across payment apps with no common record. KamaiPehchan aggregates and verifies those payments into one structured income history a lender can actually underwrite against.
